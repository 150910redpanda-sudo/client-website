This Privacy Policy explains how Discover Dentdale Ltd, trading as Dales & Lakes Holiday Cottages ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit this website or make an enquiry or booking for Gibbs Hall Barn, Dent, Cumbria.

Who we are

Information we collect

When you make an enquiry or booking, we may collect:

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited details with trusted third parties strictly where necessary, such as payment processors, or where required by law. We do not transfer your data outside the UK/EEA without appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep your information

We retain booking and enquiry records for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above and to meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations, typically no longer than seven years.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to how it is used. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Changes to this policy
