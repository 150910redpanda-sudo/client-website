Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This website, operated by Discover Dentdale Ltd trading as Dales & Lakes Holiday Cottages, uses a small amount of local storage, one optional analytics measurement and an embedded Google map. This policy explains exactly what is stored, why, and how you can change your choice at any time.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as a browser's "local storage", do the same job in a slightly different way. They can help a site function correctly, remember your preferences, or gather statistics about how the site is used.

We do not set advertising cookies, marketing pixels, social media trackers or any form of cross-site profiling ourselves. Cookies set by Google through the embedded map are used by Google under its own policies.

Your choices

When you first visit the site you are shown a banner allowing you to accept or decline analytics. Our analytics never run unless you accept, and the only thing this site itself stores is the record of your choice. The embedded Google map is not covered by this choice: it loads with the home page, and Google may set its own cookies when it does. If you would rather Google did not, you can block third-party cookies in your browser settings; the map will still display and the rest of the site works as normal. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of the home page, which will bring the banner back. Most browsers also allow you to block or delete cookies and site data through their settings.

Third parties

Two third-party services are involved in delivering this site:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the website. Like any web host, its servers process your IP address and browser information in order to deliver the pages you request, and it keeps short-lived security and traffic logs.
  • Google provides the map in the Location section of the home page. When that map loads, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and browser information and may set its own cookies, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Everything else the page needs — images, styles, scripts and typefaces — is served from this website's own domain.

Our site also links out to third-party services such as Dales & Lakes Holiday Cottages, Independent Cottages and the Yorkshire Dales National Park website. Once you follow one of those links you are on their site, governed by their own cookie and privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.
