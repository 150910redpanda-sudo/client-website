Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This website, operated by Discover Dentdale Ltd trading as Dales & Lakes Holiday Cottages, uses cookies and similar technologies. This policy explains what they are, which ones we use, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function correctly and can be used to remember your preferences or gather anonymous statistics about how the site is used.

Cookies we use

Cookie Purpose Duration
ghb_cookie_consent Remembers your cookie consent choice so the banner does not reappear on every visit. Persistent (local storage)
Analytics (if enabled) Anonymous usage statistics to help us understand how visitors use the site, only set if you accept cookies. Up to 24 months

Your choices

When you first visit the site, you will be shown a cookie banner allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's local storage and site data for this domain, which will show the banner again on your next visit. Most browsers also allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings.

Third-party links

Our site links to third-party services such as Independent Cottages and the Yorkshire Dales National Park website. These sites have their own cookie and privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.
